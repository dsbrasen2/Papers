# Papers

**Atiyah Axioms for Topological Quantum Field Theory (Oxford Tutorial):** Summary of the axioms necessary to define an abstract TQFT applied to ground state degeneracies of manifolds, systems of particles, and S-matrices.

**Inflationary Cosmology (Physics 362):** Literature review of classical inflationary cosmology, experimental evidence, and future or alternative hypotheses in the field.

**Quantum Complexity Theory (CS 254):** A survey of current topics in quantum complexity theory, and how fundamental quantum algorithms (e.g. Grover’s, Shor’s) can help to classify the complexity of quantum algorithms compared to classical ones.

**Ratio of the Sides Between a Regular Polygon and an Interior Polygon Formed by its Non-Opposite Diagonal Lines:** Novel proof determining the closed form for the side length of a polygon formed by the diagonals of a larger similar polygon. Published in MathAMATYC Journal.
